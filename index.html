About me

I am a second-year PhD student in Economics at Central European University, specializing in labour economics and econometrics under the supervision of Andrea Weber.

I also work as an economist at the Austrian Institute of Economic Research (WIFO) in the research group Labour Economics, Income and Social Security.

My research interests are labour economics, education economics, microeconometrics, and inequality.
